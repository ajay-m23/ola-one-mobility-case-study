# ola-one-mobility-case-study
Product management case study exploring a multimodal mobility platform designed to simplify daily commuting for 100 million Indians by 2035.
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem statement: Daily commuting involves unpredictable travel times, complicated transfers, and fragmented booking experiences.
Proposed solution: Ola One, a multimodal mobility platform that plans, books, and manages journeys across public transit and shared electric vehicles.
Target users: Daily commuters, students, and people who need more accessible travel options.
Product strategy: Orchestrate existing transport options instead of trying to own every mode of transportation.
Success metrics: Successful commutes, on-time arrivals, journey completion rate, and customer retention.
Key trade-offs: Infrastructure ownership versus partnerships, convenience versus cost, and coverage versus reliability.
